# Editorial policy

Deshkaal brings the world's important editorial commentary, in whatever language it appears, to readers in plain, everyday Hindustani. For every piece we write our own summary and give a link to the original, so that readers can go and read the full article there.

## Our priority

The voice of the people matters most to us. The interests of minorities, Dalits, Adivasis, denotified and nomadic (DNT) communities, women, workers, farmers and those pushed to the margins are the first test of what we choose.

## What we put first

- Communal harmony and the Ganga-Jamuni tehzeeb
- The Constitution, civil rights, freedom of expression and of the press
- Anti-caste discourse and social justice
- The rights of workers, farmers and the unorganised sector
- Environmental and climate justice, the question of water, forest and land
- The rights of LGBTQ+ and disabled people
- Federalism, linguistic diversity and regional voices
- Peace in South Asia and the Global South's point of view

## What we do not publish

- Material that spreads hate or targets any community
- Claims without evidence, and rumour
- Personal attacks
- Propaganda for any government or political party
- Sensationalist pieces
- Sources whose articles require a subscription to read

## Transparency

Every page states clearly that it is a summary, who the original author and publication are, and where the original can be read. All rights in the original articles belong to their authors and publishers. Pictures are either Deshkaal's own collages, or openly licensed, with credit.

## Correcting mistakes

If any fact in a summary is wrong, we correct it and note the correction on the page.

## Readers' work: Hamsafar

Deshkaal is at heart a people's media aggregator. Hamsafar, the page for readers' original work, is an extra feature added to it. Essays, reportage, poems, stories, cartoons and photographs can be sent to sampark@deshkaal.xyz. Details are at deshkaal.xyz/bhejiye.

- Every piece is read against this same editorial policy.
- The work should be original. If it has been published elsewhere, the author should say so, and we mention it.
- Photographs of identifiable people need their consent. We do not publish the faces of children caught in difficult situations.
- The copyright of the work stays with the author. Deshkaal has permission to publish, share and translate it.
- The author's email, phone or address is never published.
- For now we are not able to pay for any contribution.
