# Privacy policy

You do not need an account to read Deshkaal (deshkaal.xyz). We show no advertising, run no trackers or analytics code, and never sell or rent anyone's information.

## What we keep

- When you sign in: your name, email address, saved articles and favourite categories. These are kept so that your Deshkaal looks the same on your phone and your computer.
- When you join the newsletter: only your email address, so that the issue can be sent every morning.
- After you sign in, one cookie (dk_sid) stays in your browser, which tells us that you are signed in. It is our only cookie.
- To count readers we keep no cookie or personal information. Your browser holds just a mark saying you have visited before, so that you are not counted again. On the server only the total number for each day is stored, with no detail about any person.
- Colour, letter size and the article you last read stay in your own browser and do not come to us.

## Where this information lives

- The site and the readers' database are on Cloudflare.
- Emails are sent through Resend.
- Deshkaal has channels and pages on Telegram, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram. There we only post our own posts. From these places we do not take or keep any reader's personal information.

## Having information removed

- Every newsletter email has a link at the bottom with which you can unsubscribe yourself.
- To have a sign-in account and all information linked to it erased, write "Delete my data" to sampark@deshkaal.xyz. Everything will be erased within seven days and you will be told by email.

## Contact

If you have any question, write to sampark@deshkaal.xyz.
