# आमुख के लेखक (Writers for the lead)

The lead (`amukh`) is a long editorial essay or column by a writer of this stature, on the subject that matters most that day. These names are a guide, not a cage: any serious, independent thinker whose piece fits `EDITORIAL.md` may lead. Prefer a fresh piece (last 72 hours), free to read. Rotate: the same writer should not lead twice in 7 days.

## सूची से हटाये गये (Removed, with reason)
- Francis Fukuyama, Kishore Mahbubani: their framework (liberal triumphalism; defence of state power and great-power realism) runs against the editorial policy's focus on the margins and the Global South's people rather than its states.
- Jürgen Habermas: co-signed the 2023 statement defending Israel's war on Gaza; against the policy on peace and human rights.
- Manoj Jha: a sitting MP of a political party; the policy does not carry party voices.
- Abdurrahman Wahid: died in 2009, no new writing.
- Sulak Sivaraksa, Taizun Zhang: no regular columns that the run can find; use only if a fresh essay appears.
